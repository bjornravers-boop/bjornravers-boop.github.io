Literally Emergency

In an emergency, your head goes blank. One big button for the right emergency number wherever you are, your location to read out, and your medical info for whoever comes to help.

  • The right number in any country (112, 911, 999, 000…), worked out from your network.
  • Your address, coordinates and Plus Code in big text for the dispatcher.
  • Medical info on the lock screen, translated on-device for helpers.
  • Pair with family: their phone rings through silent, with end-to-end encrypted live location.

Literally Just a Scanner

You have a piece of paper. You want it to be a PDF on your phone. No account, no subscription, no watermark. It is literally just a scanner.

  • Automatic edge detection, with a crop you can fine-tune yourself.
  • Photo, Color, Grayscale and B&W filters with contrast control.
  • Combine and reorder pages into one shareable PDF.
  • Everything is processed on your device; choose where scans are saved.

Literally Plantscan

Point your camera at a plant and find out what it is. Recognition runs entirely on your phone, free and unlimited, even without a connection.

  • Works fully offline: your photos are never uploaded anywhere.
  • A private scan history you can revisit or delete at any time.
  • Trained on openly licensed photos shared by the iNaturalist community.

The internet has enough recurring fees. We aren’t going to siphon your bank account every month just so you can use a basic tool.

02

If we ever ask for money, it’s entirely optional, like tossing a coin in a digital tip jar. Never a ransom to unlock a basic feature.

03

Why name an app company after your grandma? (Yes, UllieMoeke means “Your Grandmother.”)

Our story

Because grandmothers are the ultimate standard for how things should work. They don’t lock their life advice behind a premium paywall, and they certainly don’t make you watch an unskippable ad before handing you a hard candy. They just want you to be happy, comfortable, and a little less confused by the world.

That’s exactly how we build our software.

We aren’t here to disrupt the tech industry or build the next hyper-optimized productivity suite. We make apps that solve incredibly stupid problems: the tiny, ridiculous, everyday annoyances that don’t really matter but still drive you crazy. No bloated features, no endless menus, and absolutely no stress.

We just want to make you smile, solve a dumb problem, and send you on your way.
