Are “digital cookies” tracking cookies?

No. Digital cookies are a little in-app collectible you get for watching an optional ad. They have no cash value, are stored on your device, and have nothing to do with browser tracking cookies.

Do I have to watch ads?

Never. Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one. Scanning, identifying plants and the emergency buttons always work without ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may process your IP address, device or advertising identifiers and ad interactions; each app’s policy has the details.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where applicable, open Support → Ad privacy choices in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

How do I delete my data?

Everything an app stores lives on your phone. Delete items inside the app, or clear the app’s data in Android settings to remove everything. Files you saved to a shared folder, such as Downloads, are deleted separately.

What happens when I donate?

Donations are handled by PayPal under its own privacy policy. The app never sees your card or bank details. We may receive donor and transaction details through PayPal to manage donations.

Does this website track me?

This website has no analytics, ads or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when you visit. Your light or dark mode choice and website cookie count are remembered in your own browser only.
