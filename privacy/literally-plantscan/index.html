Privacy policy

Literally Plantscan · UllieMoeke Productions · Updated 25 September 2026

Offline recognitionPlants are identified on your phone; photos are never uploaded.
No accountNo sign-up or email address needed.
Ads are optionalOnly when you choose to watch one for a cookie.

Your photos

The camera is used only to photograph plants you choose to identify. Plant identification runs entirely on your device; photos are never uploaded to our servers or any identification service. Scans in your history, including their photos, are stored privately inside the app until you delete them.

Optional advertising

The Android app uses Google AdMob for ads that you choose to watch, and Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage advertising privacy choices. These services may process your IP address (including approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Consent-related requests may occur when the app starts; ads are requested when you choose to watch one. Scanning plants does not require watching an ad.

Where applicable, you can change consent using “Ad privacy choices” in Support. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings. Google describes its data handling at Google Privacy Policy.

Optional donations

Donations open PayPal. PayPal handles your payment information under its own privacy policy. The app does not receive your card or bank details and stores only a local flag to ask whether you completed the donation. We may receive donor and transaction details through PayPal to manage donations.

Accounts, retention and choices

No account or email address is required. Your scan history, digital-cookie collection count and the donation reminder are stored locally. Digital cookies are in-app collectibles, not browser tracking cookies. Deleting a scan removes it and its photo; clearing app data removes everything. Advertising and payment providers manage their own retention. If you contact us by email, we use your message and address to respond and retain correspondence as needed to handle your request.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have provided to us: ulliemoekeproductions@gmail.com.
