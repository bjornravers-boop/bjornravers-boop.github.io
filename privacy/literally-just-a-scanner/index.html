Privacy policy

Literally Just a Scanner · UllieMoeke Productions · Updated 18 September 2026

On your deviceScans and PDFs are made on your phone, never uploaded to us.
No accountNo sign-up or email address needed.
Ads are optionalOnly when you choose to watch one for a cookie.

Your scans

Photos, document cropping and PDF creation are processed on your device. We do not upload your scans to our servers. Saved files remain in your chosen folder until you delete them. When you import from a cloud photo app or share a file, the service you choose handles that transfer under its own privacy policy.

Optional advertising

The Android app uses Google AdMob for ads that you choose to watch, and Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage advertising privacy choices. These services may process your IP address (including approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Consent-related requests may occur when the app starts; ads are requested when you choose to watch one. Scanning does not require watching an ad.

Where applicable, you can change consent using “Ad privacy choices” in Support. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings. Google describes its data handling at Google Privacy Policy.

Optional donations

Donations open PayPal. PayPal handles your payment information under its own privacy policy. The app does not receive your card or bank details and stores only a local flag to ask whether you completed the donation. We may receive donor and transaction details through PayPal to manage donations.

Accounts, retention and choices

No scanner account or email address is required. App settings, your digital-cookie collection count and the donation reminder are stored locally. Digital cookies are in-app collectibles, not browser tracking cookies. Clearing app data removes these settings and collectibles; files saved in Downloads or another shared folder must be deleted separately. Advertising and payment providers manage their own retention. If you contact us by email, we use your message and address to respond and retain correspondence as needed to handle your request.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have provided to us: ulliemoekeproductions@gmail.com.
